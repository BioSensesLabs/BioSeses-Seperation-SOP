Collect 5 tubes per participant: 4 K2 EDTA tubes (10 mL) and 1 plain SST (10 mL) unless indicated otherwise in Disease's CRF/Survey sheet. Immediately label all tubes. Gently invert EDTA tubes after collection to mix with anticoagulants. Handle plain SST tubes according to the applicable serum-processing procedure. Record collection date and time.

5 Sample Processing Checklist

Complete for Every Sample. Use one copy of Sections 5-8 for every participant/sample.

5.1 Plasma Collection and Blood Dilution

Step Action Record Done
1 Centrifuge 2 of the 4 EDTA blood tubes at 2000 rpm for 10 minutes. Unless indicated in the Disease's CRF/Survey sheet to collect plasma from all tubes.
2 Collect plasma (top layer) into a labeled tube and aliquot into 2–4 labeled cryovials, each containing 1 mL; store cryovials in the freezing container.
3 Replace the same volume removed as plasma with sterile PBS or sterile NaCl 0.9%. Invert to mix.
4 Measure and record total blood volume in all tubes.
5 Add an equal volume of sterile PBS or sterile NaCl 0.9% (1:1).
6 Mix gently by inversion. Do not vortex.
7 Centrifuge the plain SST blood collection tube at 2000 rpm for 10 min.
8 Collect serum from the serum layer. Transfer 1 mL of serum into each cryovial. ≥ 2 vials of 1 mL aliquots.
9 Store cryovial in the freezing container.

5.2 Ficoll Tube Selection and Layering

Step Action Record Done
10 Select tube according to diluted blood volume: <9 mL diluted blood → 15 mL tube; ≥20 mL diluted blood → 50 mL tube.
11 Calculate Ficoll volume as diluted blood volume ÷ 2.
12 Add the calculated Ficoll volume to the appropriate empty centrifuge tube. Label the tube for the designated sample to be used.
13 Set pipettor dispensing to gravity or the lowest speed.
14 Use suitable serological pipette: ≤9 mL diluted blood → 10 mL pipette; ≥13 mL → 25 mL pipette. Slowly layer diluted blood over Ficoll.
15 Do not exceed 30 mL diluted blood in a 50 mL tube containing 15 mL Ficoll.
16 Add diluted blood slowly down the tube wall; establish an approximately 3 mL layer dropwise, then continue with slow steady flow.
17 Confirm two distinct layers: Diluted blood on top, Ficoll at the bottom. Make sure to not disturb the interface.

5.3 Ficoll Centrifugation

Step Action Record Done
18 Set centrifuge to LOWEST acceleration and LOWEST brake.
19 Confirm centrifuge is at least room temperature or warmed to 37°C as specified in the supplied SOP.
20 Use the validated programmed centrifuge method for the instrument and double-check the selected program before START.
21 Centrifuge at 2500 rpm for 20 minutes.

5.5 PBMC Collection

Step Action Record Done
22 Insert pipette tip carefully through the plasma layer.
23 Collect ONLY the cloudy PBMC interface layer.
24 Avoid collecting Ficoll or red cells.
25 Transfer PBMCs to a new labeled 50 mL tube.

5.6 First Wash

Step Action Record Done
26 Fill the tube to 50 mL with PBS.
27 Use HIGHEST acceleration and HIGHEST brake.
28 Centrifuge at 1700 rpm for 10 minutes at 4°C.
29 Discard supernatant carefully without disturbing the pellet.
30 Resuspend pellet in PBS equal to half the tube fill volume (25 mL cold PBS when using a 50 mL tube).

5.7 Second Wash

Step Action Record Done
31 Use MIDDLE acceleration and MIDDLE brake.
32 Centrifuge at 600 rpm for 10 minutes at 4°C.
33 Carefully aspirate supernatant without disturbing the pellet.

5.8 Final Resuspension

Step Action Record Done
34 Resuspend pellet in 10 mL PBS or normal saline.
35 Mix gently.

5.9 Cell Counting and QC

Step Action Record Done
36 Keep cell suspension on ice or at 4°C throughout counting process.
37 Into a separate labeled Eppendorf tube: Add 20 µL of the cell suspension.
38 Into the labeled Eppendorf tube, add 20 µL of 0.4% Trypan blue solution (one to one ratio) and gently mix the cells.
39 Load 10 µL of the cell suspension - Trypan blue mixture into the hemocytometer chamber. If overcrowded perform dilution as needed.
40 Count unstained cells as viable cells and blue stained as non-viable/dead cells.
41 Count 3 large diagonal boxes (Each containing 9 small boxes).
Box 1 Viable: Box 1 Non-Viable: Box 2 Viable: Box 2 Non-Viable: Box 3 Viable: Box 3 Non-Viable:
42 Average the viable & non-viable cell counts across 3 counted boxes.
Cell Viability (%) = (Avg Viable / Total Cells) × 100

5.10 Preparation for Freezing

Step Action Record Done
44 Centrifuge the 10 mL cell suspension at 1500 rpm at 4°C for 5 minutes.
45 Decant supernatant without reverting the tube. Keep tilted and flip tube onto a clean alcohol-sprayed paper towel.
46 Calculate volume needed to resuspend cells at a concentration of 10 million cells/mL. Calculation: ÷ 10,000,000 = -- mL
47 Confirm freezing container is pre-chilled.
48 Confirm MACS Freezing Solution is cold and ready to use.

5.11 Cryopreservation and Storage

Step Action Record Done
49 Resuspend cell pellet with calculated volume using MACS Freezing Solution.
50 Aliquot cell suspension into cryovials each containing 1mL. Determine number of full cryovials and any partial aliquot.
51 Place cryovials in isopropanol freezing container.
52 Store at -80°C for 24 hours.
53 After 24 hours, transfer cryovials to liquid nitrogen.

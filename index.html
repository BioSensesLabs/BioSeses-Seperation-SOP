2. Sample Identification and Chain of Custody[cite: 2]

Collect 6 tubes per participant — 4 EDTA tubes (4 mL) and 2 plain tubes (8 mL). Immediately label all tubes. Gently invert EDTA tubes after collection to mix with anticoagulants. Handle plain tubes according to the applicable serum-processing procedure. Record collection date and time[cite: 2].

5. Sample Processing Checklist — Complete for Every Sample[cite: 2]

Use one copy of Sections 5–8 for every participant/sample. Do not use a generic batch checklist in place of the individual sample record[cite: 2].

Sample ID[cite: 2] Collection Time[cite: 2] Processing Start[cite: 2] Processing End[cite: 2] Operator[cite: 2]

5.1 Plasma Collection and Blood Dilution[cite: 2]

Step[cite: 2] Action[cite: 2] Record[cite: 2] Done[cite: 2]
1[cite: 2] Centrifuge 2 of the 4 EDTA blood tubes at 2000 rpm for 10 minutes.[cite: 2]
2[cite: 2] Collect plasma (top layer) into a labeled tube and aliquot into at least 4 labeled cryovials, each containing 1 mL; store cryovials in the freezing container.[cite: 2]
3[cite: 2] Replace the same volume removed as plasma with sterile PBS or sterile NaCl 0.9%. Invert to mix.[cite: 2]
4[cite: 2] Measure and record total blood volume in all tubes.[cite: 2]
5[cite: 2] Add an equal volume of sterile PBS or sterile NaCl 0.9% (1:1).[cite: 2]
6[cite: 2] Mix gently by inversion. Do not vortex.[cite: 2]
7[cite: 2] Centrifuge the plain SST blood collection tube at 2000 rpm for 10 min[cite: 2]
8[cite: 2] Collect serum from the serum layer. Transfer 1 mL of serum in each cryovial and 1 ml in a test tube for biochemical and immunological test.[cite: 2]
9[cite: 2] Store cryovial in the freezing container[cite: 2]

5.2 Ficoll Tube Selection and Layering[cite: 2]

Step[cite: 2] Action[cite: 2] Record[cite: 2] Done[cite: 2]
10[cite: 2] Select tube according to diluted blood volume: ≤9 mL diluted blood → 15 mL tube; ≥20 mL diluted blood → 50 mL tube.[cite: 2]
11[cite: 2] Calculate Ficoll volume as diluted blood volume ÷ 2.[cite: 2]
12[cite: 2] Add the calculated Ficoll volume to an empty centrifuge tube.[cite: 2]
13[cite: 2] Set pipettor dispensing to gravity or the lowest speed.[cite: 2]
14[cite: 2] Use suitable serological pipette: ≤9 mL diluted blood → 10 mL pipette; ≥13 mL → 25 mL pipette. Slowly layer diluted blood over Ficoll.[cite: 2]
15[cite: 2] Do not exceed 30 mL diluted blood in a 50 mL tube containing 15 mL Ficoll.[cite: 2]
16[cite: 2] Add diluted blood slowly down the tube wall; establish an approximately 3 mL layer dropwise, then continue with slow steady flow.[cite: 2]
17[cite: 2] Confirm two distinct layers: diluted blood on top and Ficoll at the bottom. Do not disturb the interface.[cite: 2]
Layering failure control: If the layers mix, stop. The supplied SOP specifies adding approximately 50% of the total volume of PBS or saline and restarting the separation. Record the deviation/recovery in Section 8.[cite: 2]

5.3 Ficoll Centrifugation[cite: 2]

Step[cite: 2] Action[cite: 2] Record[cite: 2] Done[cite: 2]
18[cite: 2] Set centrifuge to LOWEST acceleration and LOWEST brake.[cite: 2]
19[cite: 2] Confirm centrifuge is at least room temperature or warmed to 37°C as specified in the supplied SOP.[cite: 2]
20[cite: 2] Centrifuge at 2500 rpm for 20 minutes.[cite: 2]
21[cite: 2] Use the validated programmed centrifuge method for the instrument and double-check the selected program before START.[cite: 2]

5.4 Separation QC Check[cite: 2]

QC Check[cite: 2] Result / Time / Initials[cite: 2]
Plasma top layer visible[cite: 2]
PBMC interface visible as a thin cloudy white layer[cite: 2]
Ficoll layer visible[cite: 2]
RBC/granulocyte layer visible at bottom[cite: 2]
PBMC interface suitable for collection[cite: 2]
If PBMC layer is not visible: supervisor notified[cite: 2]

5.5 PBMC Collection[cite: 2]

Step[cite: 2] Action[cite: 2] Record[cite: 2] Done[cite: 2]
22[cite: 2] Insert pipette tip carefully through the plasma layer.[cite: 2]
23[cite: 2] Collect ONLY the cloudy PBMC interface layer.[cite: 2]
24[cite: 2] Avoid collecting Ficoll or red cells.[cite: 2]
25[cite: 2] Transfer PBMCs to a new labeled 50 mL tube.[cite: 2]

5.6 First Wash[cite: 2]

Step[cite: 2] Action[cite: 2] Record[cite: 2] Done[cite: 2]
26[cite: 2] Fill the tube to 50 mL with PBS.[cite: 2]
27[cite: 2] Use HIGHEST acceleration and HIGHEST brake.[cite: 2]
28[cite: 2] Centrifuge at 1700 rpm for 10 minutes at 4°C.[cite: 2]
29[cite: 2] Discard supernatant carefully without disturbing the pellet.[cite: 2]
30[cite: 2] Resuspend pellet in PBS equal to half the tube fill volume (25 mL cold PBS when using a 50 mL tube).[cite: 2]

5.7 Second Wash[cite: 2]

Step[cite: 2] Action[cite: 2] Record[cite: 2] Done[cite: 2]
31[cite: 2] Use MIDDLE acceleration and MIDDLE brake.[cite: 2]
32[cite: 2] Centrifuge at 600 rpm for 10 minutes at 4°C.[cite: 2]
33[cite: 2] Carefully aspirate supernatant without disturbing the pellet. Double-check the correct program before START.[cite: 2]

5.8 Final Resuspension[cite: 2]

Step[cite: 2] Action[cite: 2] Record[cite: 2] Done[cite: 2]
34[cite: 2] Resuspend pellet in 10 mL PBS or normal saline.[cite: 2]
35[cite: 2] Mix gently.[cite: 2]

5.9 Cell Counting and QC[cite: 2]

Step[cite: 2] Action[cite: 2] Record[cite: 2] Done[cite: 2]
36[cite: 2] Label the cell suspension for each subject under study.[cite: 2]
37[cite: 2] Add 20 uL cell suspension to 20 uL of 0.4% Trypan blue solution (one to one ratio) and gently mix the cells.[cite: 2]
38[cite: 2] Load 10-20 uL drop of the cell–Trypan blue mixture into the haemocytometer chamber.[cite: 2]
39[cite: 2] Count unstained cells as viable cells and stained cells as non-viable/dead cells. Count a total of at least 200 cells. Cells within the diagonal boxes should be included in the count for both viable and non-viable cells, regardless of the number of cells present.[cite: 2]
*If the cell overcrowded perform further dilution and account for the additional dilution factor in calculation[cite: 2]

5.10 Preparation for Freezing[cite: 2]

Step[cite: 2] Action[cite: 2] Record[cite: 2] Done[cite: 2]
42[cite: 2] Confirm both cryovials and freezing containers are pre-chilled.[cite: 2]
43[cite: 2] Centrifuge cells at 1500 rpm for 5 minutes.[cite: 2]
44[cite: 2] Decant the supernatant without inverting the tube. Keep the tube tilted and remove any remaining liquid onto a clean, alcohol-sprayed paper towel. Do not exceed 1–2 minutes.[cite: 2]
45[cite: 2] Using the total viable cell count from Step 39, calculate the volume of ready-to-use MACS Freezing Solution required to achieve a final cell concentration of 1 × 10⁷ cells/mL.[cite: 2] Target: 10,000,000 cells/mL[cite: 2]
46[cite: 2] Prepare the calculated final suspension volume using MACS Freezing Solution.[cite: 2]
47[cite: 2] Determine number of full cryovials and any partial aliquot.[cite: 2]
48[cite: 2] Keep cell suspension on ice or at 4°C.[cite: 2]

5.11 Cryopreservation and Storage[cite: 2]

Step[cite: 2] Action[cite: 2] Record[cite: 2] Done[cite: 2]
49[cite: 2] Aliquot 1.0 mL of prepared cell suspension directly into each cryovial.[cite: 2]
50[cite: 2] Each cryovial must contain 10 million PBMCs[cite: 2]
51[cite: 2] Mix gently by inversion. Do not vortex.[cite: 2]
52[cite: 2] Confirm final vial volume is 1.0 mL.[cite: 2]
53[cite: 2] Place cryovials in an isopropanol freezing container.[cite: 2]
54[cite: 2] Store at −80°C for 24 hours.[cite: 2]
55[cite: 2] After 24 hours, transfer cryovials to liquid nitrogen.[cite: 2]

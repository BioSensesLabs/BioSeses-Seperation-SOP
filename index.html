5.1 Plasma Collection and Blood Dilution

Step Action Record Done
1 Centrifuge 2 of the 4 EDTA blood tubes at 2000 rpm for 10 minutes.
2 Collect plasma (top layer) into a labeled tube and aliquot into 2–4 labeled cryovials.
3 Replace the same volume removed as plasma with sterile PBS or sterile NaCl 0.9%.
4 Measure and record total blood volume in all tubes.
5 Add an equal volume of sterile PBS or sterile NaCl 0.9% (1:1).
6 Mix gently by inversion. Do not vortex.
7 Centrifuge the plain SST blood collection tube at 2000 rpm for 10 min
8 Collect serum from the serum layer. Transfer 1 mL of serum into each cryovial.
9 Store cryovial in the freezing container

5.2 Ficoll Tube Selection and Layering

Step Action Record Done
10 Select tube according to diluted blood volume.
11 Calculate Ficoll volume as diluted blood volume ÷ 2.
12 Add the calculated Ficoll volume to the appropriate empty centrifuge tube.
13 Set pipettor dispensing to gravity or the lowest speed.
14 Use suitable serological pipette. Slowly layer diluted blood over Ficoll.
15 Do not exceed 30 mL diluted blood in a 50 mL tube.
16 Add diluted blood slowly down the tube wall.
17 Confirm two distinct layers: Diluted blood on top, Ficoll at bottom.

5.3 Ficoll Centrifugation

Step Action Record Done
18 Set centrifuge to LOWEST acceleration and LOWEST brake.
19 Confirm centrifuge temperature.
20 Use the validated programmed centrifuge method.
21 Centrifuge at 2500 rpm for 20 minutes.

5.5 PBMC Collection

Step Action Record Done
22 Insert pipette tip carefully through plasma layer.
23 Collect ONLY the cloudy PBMC interface layer.
24 Avoid collecting Ficoll or red cells.
25 Transfer PBMCs to a new labeled 50 mL tube.

5.6 First Wash

Step Action Record Done
26 Fill the tube to 50 mL with PBS.
27 Use HIGHEST acceleration and HIGHEST brake.
28 Centrifuge at 1700 rpm for 10 minutes at 4°C.
29 Discard supernatant carefully without disturbing pellet.
30 Resuspend pellet in cold PBS.

5.7 Second Wash

Step Action Record Done
31 Use MIDDLE acceleration and MIDDLE brake.
32 Centrifuge at 600 rpm for 10 minutes at 4°C.
33 Carefully aspirate supernatant without disturbing pellet.

5.8 Final Resuspension

Step Action Record Done
34 Resuspend pellet in 10 mL PBS or normal saline.
35 Mix gently.

5.9 Cell Counting and QC

Step Action Record Done
36 Keep cell suspension on ice or at 4°C.
37 Add 20 µL of cell suspension to Eppendorf.
38 Add 20 µL of 0.4% Trypan blue solution (1:1 ratio).
39 Load 10 µL into hemocytometer chamber.
40 Count unstained as viable, blue as non-viable. Viable = Unstained | Non-viable = Blue
41 Count 3 large diagonal boxes.
Box 1 Viable: Box 1 Non-Viable: Box 2 Viable: Box 2 Non-Viable: Box 3 Viable: Box 3 Non-Viable:
42 Average counts & calculate viability.
Viability: -- %
43 Calculate lymphocyte concentration & total yield.
Conc: -- cells/mL
Total Yield: -- cells

5.10 Preparation for Freezing

Step Action Record Done
44 Centrifuge cell suspension at 1500 rpm at 4°C for 5 minutes.
45 Decant supernatant & remove remaining liquid.
46 Calculate volume needed for 10M cells/mL.
MACS Media Vol: -- mL
47 Confirm freezing container is pre-chilled.
48 Confirm MACS Freezing Solution is cold and ready.

5.11 Cryopreservation and Storage

Step Action Record Done
49 Resuspend pellet with MACS Freezing Solution.
50 Aliquot cell suspension into cryovials (1 mL each).
51 Place cryovials in isopropanol freezing container.
52 Store at -80°C for 24 hours.
53 Transfer cryovials to liquid nitrogen.

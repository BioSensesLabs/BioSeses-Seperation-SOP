2. Sample Identification and Chain of Custody[cite: 1]

Collection Note[cite: 1]: Collect 6 tubes per participant — 4 EDTA tubes (4 mL) and 2 plain tubes (8 mL). Immediately label all tubes. Gently invert EDTA tubes after collection to mix with anticoagulants. Handle plain tubes according to the applicable serum-processing procedure. Record collection date and time[cite: 1].

5. Sample Processing Checklist — Complete for Every Sample[cite: 1]

Use one copy of Sections 5–8 for every participant/sample. Do not use a generic batch checklist in place of the individual sample record[cite: 1].

5.1 Plasma Collection and Blood Dilution[cite: 1]

Step[cite: 1] Action[cite: 1] Record[cite: 1] Done[cite: 1]
1[cite: 1] Centrifuge 2 of the 4 EDTA blood tubes at 2000 rpm for 10 minutes[cite: 1].
2[cite: 1] Collect plasma (top layer) into a labeled tube and aliquot into at least 4 labeled cryovials, each containing 1 mL; store cryovials in the freezing container[cite: 1].
3[cite: 1] Replace the same volume removed as plasma with sterile PBS or sterile NaCl 0.9%. Invert to mix[cite: 1].
4[cite: 1] Measure and record total blood volume in all tubes[cite: 1].
5[cite: 1] Add an equal volume of sterile PBS or sterile NaCl 0.9% (1:1)[cite: 1].
6[cite: 1] Mix gently by inversion. Do not vortex[cite: 1].
7[cite: 1] Centrifuge the plain SST blood collection tube at 2000 rpm for 10 min[cite: 1].
8[cite: 1] Collect serum from the serum layer. Transfer 1 mL of serum in each cryovial and 1 ml in a test tube for biochemical and immunological test[cite: 1].
9[cite: 1] Store cryovial in the freezing container[cite: 1].

5.2 Ficoll Tube Selection and Layering[cite: 1]

Step[cite: 1] Action[cite: 1] Record[cite: 1] Done[cite: 1]
10[cite: 1] Select tube according to diluted blood volume: ≤9 mL diluted blood → 15 mL tube; ≥20 mL diluted blood → 50 mL tube[cite: 1].
11[cite: 1] Calculate Ficoll volume as diluted blood volume ÷ 2[cite: 1].
12[cite: 1] Add the calculated Ficoll volume to an empty centrifuge tube[cite: 1].
13[cite: 1] Set pipettor dispensing to gravity or the lowest speed[cite: 1].
14[cite: 1] Use suitable serological pipette: ≤9 mL diluted blood → 10 mL pipette; ≥13 mL → 25 mL pipette. Slowly layer diluted blood over Ficoll[cite: 1].
15[cite: 1] Do not exceed 30 mL diluted blood in a 50 mL tube containing 15 mL Ficoll[cite: 1].
16[cite: 1] Add diluted blood slowly down the tube wall; establish an approximately 3 mL layer dropwise, then continue with slow steady flow[cite: 1].
17[cite: 1] Confirm two distinct layers: diluted blood on top and Ficoll at the bottom. Do not disturb the interface[cite: 1].
Layering failure control[cite: 1]: If the layers mix, stop. The supplied SOP specifies adding approximately 50% of the total volume of PBS or saline and restarting the separation. Record the deviation/recovery in Section 8[cite: 1].

5.3 Ficoll Centrifugation[cite: 1]

Step[cite: 1] Action[cite: 1] Record[cite: 1] Done[cite: 1]
18[cite: 1] Set centrifuge to LOWEST acceleration and LOWEST brake[cite: 1].
19[cite: 1] Confirm centrifuge is at least room temperature or warmed to 37°C as specified in the supplied SOP[cite: 1].
20[cite: 1] Centrifuge at 2500 rpm for 20 minutes[cite: 1].
21[cite: 1] Use the validated programmed centrifuge method for the instrument and double-check the selected program before START[cite: 1].

5.4 Separation QC Check[cite: 1]

QC Check[cite: 1] Result / Time / Initials[cite: 1]
Plasma top layer visible[cite: 1]
PBMC interface visible as a thin cloudy white layer[cite: 1]
Ficoll layer visible[cite: 1]
RBC/granulocyte layer visible at bottom[cite: 1]
PBMC interface suitable for collection[cite: 1]
If PBMC layer is not visible: supervisor notified[cite: 1]

5.5 PBMC Collection[cite: 1]

Step[cite: 1] Action[cite: 1] Record[cite: 1] Done[cite: 1]
22[cite: 1] Insert pipette tip carefully through the plasma layer[cite: 1].
23[cite: 1] Collect ONLY the cloudy PBMC interface layer[cite: 1].
24[cite: 1] Avoid collecting Ficoll or red cells[cite: 1].
25[cite: 1] Transfer PBMCs to a new labeled 50 mL tube[cite: 1].

5.6 First Wash[cite: 1]

Step[cite: 1] Action[cite: 1] Record[cite: 1] Done[cite: 1]
26[cite: 1] Fill the tube to 50 mL with PBS[cite: 1].
27[cite: 1] Use HIGHEST acceleration and HIGHEST brake[cite: 1].
28[cite: 1] Centrifuge at 1700 rpm for 10 minutes at 4°C[cite: 1].
29[cite: 1] Discard supernatant carefully without disturbing the pellet[cite: 1].
30[cite: 1] Resuspend pellet in PBS equal to half the tube fill volume (25 mL cold PBS when using a 50 mL tube)[cite: 1].

5.7 Second Wash[cite: 1]

Step[cite: 1] Action[cite: 1] Record[cite: 1] Done[cite: 1]
31[cite: 1] Use MIDDLE acceleration and MIDDLE brake[cite: 1].
32[cite: 1] Centrifuge at 600 rpm for 10 minutes at 4°C[cite: 1].
33[cite: 1] Carefully aspirate supernatant without disturbing the pellet. Double-check the correct program before START[cite: 1].

5.8 Final Resuspension[cite: 1]

Step[cite: 1] Action[cite: 1] Record[cite: 1] Done[cite: 1]
34[cite: 1] Resuspend pellet in 10 mL PBS or normal saline[cite: 1].
35[cite: 1] Mix gently[cite: 1].

5.9 Cell Counting and QC[cite: 1]

Step[cite: 1] Action[cite: 1] Record[cite: 1] Done[cite: 1]
36[cite: 1] Label the cell suspension for each subject under study[cite: 1].
37[cite: 1] Add 20 uL cell suspension to 20 uL of 0.4% Trypan blue solution (one to one ratio) and gently mix the cells[cite: 1].
38[cite: 1] Load 10-20 uL drop of the cell–Trypan blue mixture into the haemocytometer chamber[cite: 1].
39[cite: 1] Count unstained cells as viable cells and stained cells as non-viable/dead cells. Count a total of at least 200 cells. Cells within the diagonal boxes should be included in the count for both viable and non-viable cells, regardless of the number of cells present[cite: 1].
*If the cell overcrowded perform further dilution and account for the additional dilution factor in calculation[cite: 1]
40[cite: 1] Calculate cell viability (%): Cell Viability (%) = (Viable Cells ÷ Total Cells) × 100[cite: 1]

5.10 Preparation for Freezing[cite: 1]

Step[cite: 1] Action[cite: 1] Record[cite: 1] Done[cite: 1]
42[cite: 1] Confirm both cryovials and freezing containers are pre-chilled[cite: 1].
43[cite: 1] Centrifuge cells at 1500 rpm for 5 minutes[cite: 1].
44[cite: 1] Decant the supernatant without inverting the tube. Keep the tube tilted and remove any remaining liquid onto a clean, alcohol-sprayed paper towel. Do not exceed 1–2 minutes[cite: 1].
45[cite: 1] Using the total viable cell count from Step 39, calculate the volume of ready-to-use MACS Freezing Solution required to achieve a final cell concentration of 1 × 10⁷ cells/mL[cite: 1].
46[cite: 1] Prepare the calculated final suspension volume using MACS Freezing Solution[cite: 1].
47[cite: 1] Determine number of full cryovials and any partial aliquot[cite: 1].
48[cite: 1] Keep cell suspension on ice or at 4°C[cite: 1].

5.11 Cryopreservation and Storage[cite: 1]

Step[cite: 1] Action[cite: 1] Record[cite: 1] Done[cite: 1]
49[cite: 1] Aliquot 1.0 mL of prepared cell suspension directly into each cryovial[cite: 1].
50[cite: 1] Each cryovial must contain 10 million PBMCs[cite: 1].
51[cite: 1] Mix gently by inversion. Do not vortex[cite: 1].
52[cite: 1] Confirm final vial volume is 1.0 mL[cite: 1].
53[cite: 1] Place cryovials in an isopropanol freezing container[cite: 1].
54[cite: 1] Store at −80°C for 24 hours[cite: 1].
55[cite: 1] After 24 hours, transfer cryovials to liquid nitrogen[cite: 1].

PBMC Isolation and Cryopreservation - SOP Checklist

1. Critical Rules — Mandatory Before Processing

2. Sample Identification and Chain of Custody

Collect 6 tubes per participant — 4 EDTA tubes (4 mL) and 2 plain tubes (8 mL). Immediately label all tubes. Gently invert EDTA tubes after collection to mix with anticoagulants. Handle plain tubes according to the applicable serum-processing procedure. Record collection date and time.

5. Sample Processing Checklist — Complete for Every Sample

Use one copy of Sections 5–8 for every participant/sample. Do not use a generic batch checklist in place of the individual sample record.

5.1 Plasma Collection and Blood Dilution

Step Action Record Done
1 Centrifuge 2 of the 4 EDTA blood tubes at 2000 rpm for 10 minutes.
2 Collect plasma (top layer) into a labeled tube and aliquot into at least 4 labeled cryovials, each containing 1 mL; store cryovials in the freezing container.
3 Replace the same volume removed as plasma with sterile PBS or sterile NaCl 0.9%. Invert to mix.
4 Measure and record total blood volume in all tubes.
5 Add an equal volume of sterile PBS or sterile NaCl 0.9% (1:1).
6 Mix gently by inversion. Do not vortex.
7 Centrifuge the plain SST blood collection tube at 2000 rpm for 10 min.
8 Collect serum from the serum layer. Transfer 1 mL of serum in each cryovial and 1 ml in a test tube for biochemical and immunological test.
9 Store cryovial in the freezing container.

5.2 Ficoll Tube Selection and Layering

Step Action Record Done
10 Select tube according to diluted blood volume: ≤9 mL diluted blood → 15 mL tube; ≥20 mL diluted blood → 50 mL tube.
11 Calculate Ficoll volume as diluted blood volume ÷ 2.
12 Add the calculated Ficoll volume to an empty centrifuge tube.
13 Set pipettor dispensing to gravity or the lowest speed.
14 Use suitable serological pipette: ≤9 mL diluted blood → 10 mL pipette; ≥13 mL → 25 mL pipette. Slowly layer diluted blood over Ficoll.
15 Do not exceed 30 mL diluted blood in a 50 mL tube containing 15 mL Ficoll.
16 Add diluted blood slowly down the tube wall; establish an approximately 3 mL layer dropwise, then continue with slow steady flow.
17 Confirm two distinct layers: diluted blood on top and Ficoll at the bottom. Do not disturb the interface.
Layering failure control: If the layers mix, stop. The supplied SOP specifies adding approximately 50% of the total volume of PBS or saline and restarting the separation. Record the deviation/recovery in Section 8.

5.3 Ficoll Centrifugation

Step Action Record Done
18 Set centrifuge to LOWEST acceleration and LOWEST brake.
19 Confirm centrifuge is at least room temperature or warmed to 37°C as specified in the supplied SOP.
20 Centrifuge at 2500 rpm for 20 minutes.
21 Use the validated programmed centrifuge method for the instrument and double-check the selected program before START.

5.5 PBMC Collection

Step Action Record Done
22 Insert pipette tip carefully through the plasma layer.
23 Collect ONLY the cloudy PBMC interface layer.
24 Avoid collecting Ficoll or red cells.
25 Transfer PBMCs to a new labeled 50 mL tube.

5.6 First Wash

Step Action Record Done
26 Fill the tube to 50 mL with PBS.
27 Use HIGHEST acceleration and HIGHEST brake.
28 Centrifuge at 1700 rpm for 10 minutes at 4°C.
29 Discard supernatant carefully without disturbing the pellet.
30 Resuspend pellet in PBS equal to half the tube fill volume (25 mL cold PBS when using a 50 mL tube).

5.7 Second Wash

Step Action Record Done
31 Use MIDDLE acceleration and MIDDLE brake.
32 Centrifuge at 600 rpm for 10 minutes at 4°C.
33 Carefully aspirate supernatant without disturbing the pellet. Double-check the correct program before START.

5.8 Final Resuspension

Step Action Record Done
34 Resuspend pellet in 10 mL PBS or normal saline.
35 Mix gently.

5.9 Cell Counting and QC

Step Action Record Done
36 Label the cell suspension for each subject under study.
37 Add 20 uL cell suspension to 20 uL of 0.4% Trypan blue solution (one to one ratio) and gently mix the cells.
38 Load 10-20 uL drop of the cell–Trypan blue mixture into the haemocytometer chamber.
39 Count unstained cells as viable cells and stained cells as non-viable/dead cells. Count a total of at least 200 cells. Cells within diagonal boxes should be included in count.
*If cell overcrowded perform further dilution and account for additional dilution factor in calculation
40 Calculate cell viability (%).
Cell Viability (%) = (Viable Cells ÷ Total Cells) × 100
Viability: -- %
41 Calculate lymphocyte concentration using:
Cells/mL = (Total cells ÷ Squares counted) × 10⁴ × Dilution factor
Cells/mL: --
MACS Vol Req: -- mL

5.10 Preparation for Freezing

Step Action Record Done
42 Confirm both cryovials and freezing containers are pre-chilled.
43 Centrifuge cells at 1500 rpm for 5 minutes.
44 Decant supernatant without inverting tube. Keep tube tilted onto paper towel. Do not exceed 1–2 minutes.
45 Using total viable cell count from Step 39, calculate MACS Freezing Solution required to achieve 1 × 10⁷ cells/mL.
Target: 10,000,000 cells/mL
46 Prepare calculated final suspension volume using MACS Freezing Solution.
47 Determine number of full cryovials and any partial aliquot.
48 Keep cell suspension on ice or at 4°C.

5.11 Cryopreservation and Storage

Step Action Record Done
49 Aliquot 1.0 mL of prepared cell suspension directly into each cryovial.
50 Each cryovial must contain 10 million PBMCs.
51 Mix gently by inversion. Do not vortex.
52 Confirm final vial volume is 1.0 mL.
53 Place cryovials in an isopropanol freezing container.
54 Store at −80°C for 24 hours.
55 After 24 hours, transfer cryovials to liquid nitrogen.

9. Troubleshooting

Issue Check / Action from supplied SOP Record
No PBMC layer visible Check whether blood/Ficoll mixed, whether brake was used, and whether the spin speed was correct. Notify supervisor.
PBMC layer/interface disrupted Stop. Follow the recovery instruction specified in this SOP and document the deviation.
Pellet lost during aspiration Aspirate slowly from the tube wall; document the event.
Low viability Check whether reagents were warmed too long, processing was delayed, or mixing was rough; document findings.

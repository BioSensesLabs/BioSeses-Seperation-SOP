Instructions: Collect 6 tubes per participant — 4 EDTA tubes (4 mL) and 2 plain tubes (8 mL). Immediately label all tubes. Gently invert EDTA tubes after collection to mix with anticoagulants. 9. Troubleshooting Section 9
Issue Check / Action from supplied SOP Record
No PBMC layer visible Check whether blood/Ficoll mixed, whether brake was used, and whether the spin speed was correct. Notify supervisor.
PBMC layer/interface disrupted Stop. Follow the recovery instruction specified in this SOP and document the deviation.
Pellet lost during aspiration Aspirate slowly from the tube wall; document the event.
Low viability Check whether reagents were warmed too long, processing was delayed, or mixing was rough; document findings.
